<p>This website was built using Jekyll to demonstrate a modern, sectioned personal portfolio layout.</p>

<h3>Goals Achieved</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Implemented 5 major sections (Home, Blog, Projects, Roblox, About).</li>
    <li>Used distinct collections and taxonomies for separation.</li>
    <li>Configured advanced plugins including jekyll-seo-tag, jekyll-feed, etc.</li>
</ul>

<p>The primary goal was to demonstrate clean, maintainable Jekyll architecture.</p
